I work on **Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Human-Centered AI**. My research improves human–AI alignment in open-ended creative domains, enabling AI systems to better serve individual users' goals and values. I pursue this through two complementary threads — alignment through *interaction* and alignment through *training*: **(1) designing new human–AI interaction paradigms** — constructing common ground between humans and AI at use time to make ambiguous intent explicit and model behavior inspectable; and **(2) integrating human insights into AI models** — eliciting the tacit knowledge, constraints, and judgment of domain experts and translating them into computational representations for model training and fine-tuning. 
<!-- Together, carefully designed interaction aligns AI behavior with each individual user at use time, while training on human-derived signals embeds their expertise and preference into models before deployment. -->
